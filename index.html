Programs

Two trades. One standard: site-ready.

Each program is built around the tasks an inspector or foreman will actually put in front of you on day one.

Program 01 — Scaffolding

Scaffolding Erection & Dismantling

Build and take down tube-and-coupler and frame scaffolds the way inspectors actually check them — sound footings, complete bracing, correct tagging.

  • Erection & dismantling procedure
  • Load classes & safe working limits
  • Fall protection & anchorage
  • Tag-out & inspection tagging
  • Base plates, sole boards & footings
Program 02 — Pipefitting

Pipefitting

Read the drawing, cut and fit the run, and leave a joint that passes inspection the first time.

  • Blueprint & isometric reading
  • Pipe layout & measurement
  • Cutting, threading & grooving
  • Fit-up & alignment
  • Joining methods & leak testing
